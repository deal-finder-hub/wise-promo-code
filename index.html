Wise referral FAQs

What is the Wise referral code?

MARCK841 is the code in the personal invitation link.

Do I need to enter the code manually?

Usually no. Open the invitation link before registering. If prompted for an invite code, enter MARCK841.

Can I get both offers?

The supplied guide describes a choice: a fee-free first transfer up to €500 or a free physical card order, depending on eligibility and country.

How long does the transfer offer last?

The supplied guide says the fee-free transfer benefit can expire six months after signup. Verify the offer shown in your Wise account.

Who is eligible?

The supplied guide specifies new customers aged 18 or older, subject to country availability and programme conditions.

Are third-party charges included?

No. The source says external charges, such as certain taxes, are not waived.
